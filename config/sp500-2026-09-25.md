# S&P 500 constituents as of 2026-09-25

`demo-bundle-v2` trades the S&P 500 as it stood on 2026-09-25: 503 symbols (500 companies plus share
classes), each with company name, GICS sector and SEC CIK. SPY is the benchmark and is not in the
list; `sp500-sources.toml` names it under `[universe] benchmarks`.

The list itself is not committed, because this repository is public. It lives at
`data/raw/universe/sp500-2026-09-25.csv` (gitignored), where `sp500-sources.toml` points, and its
names, sectors and CIKs are stored only in the market database. `sp500-universe.example.csv` shows
the format with made-up rows. Pass another location with `--universe-csv`.

## Regenerating the CSV

1. Fetch the revision's rendered HTML:
   `https://en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php?action=parse&oldid=1376729338&prop=text&format=json&formatversion=2`,
   with a User-Agent that names a contact.
2. Read the table with `id="constituents"`. Keep its Symbol, Security, GICS Sector and CIK columns, as
   `symbol,name,gics_sector,cik`, with the CIK as an integer (no leading zeros). Sort by symbol.
3. Write it to `data/raw/universe/sp500-2026-09-25.csv` with the header
   `symbol,name,gics_sector,cik`. `load_config` refuses a row with any empty field, so a missing
   sector stops the build instead of being guessed.
4. Check it: 503 rows; every CIK matches SEC's `company_tickers.json` for the same ticker (read `.` as
   `-`); the symbols match fja05680's members on 2026-09-07 apart from the September rebalance.

## Reconciliation (2026-10-11)

- fja05680 on 2026-09-07 and the Wikipedia revision current then (1373104626) list the same 503
  symbols.
- The September rebalance (three additions and three removals) falls after fja05680's last commit,
  so only Wikipedia covers it.
- All 503 CIKs match SEC. Every row has a GICS sector from Wikipedia.
- Share classes use the dot form (BRK.B, BF.B), which is what Alpaca's bars use. Alpaca's news tags
  them differently, so `[universe] news_aliases` fetches BRK/B and BRK for BRK.B, and BF for BF.B.

## Inside the window

The universe stays as of 2026-09-25. Between revisions 1376729338 and 1379247819:

- Paramount Skydance changed ticker from PSKY to SKYD on 2026-10-06. `[universe] renames` stores
  SKYD's bars from that day as PSKY, and the import records it in `data_ticker_changes`.
- Two constituents left the index and two other companies joined. One that left, WBD, has no bar
  after 2026-10-05, so `import-universe` excludes it (see `data_universe_exclusions`).

Constituents that changed ticker or listed during the bars lookback keep only their current ticker's
history; none changed on or after 2026-09-21, the first session the window's 5-day changes need.
